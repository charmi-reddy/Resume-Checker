# Resume Checker

A smart tool that scans your resume and gives instant feedback to help you improve readability, structure, and overall quality — all from your browser.

**Live Demo:** https://resume-checker-6996.streamlit.app/

---

## 🚀 What It Does

Resume Checker analyzes your uploaded resume and returns actionable insights on structure, clarity, and formatting.  
It helps you make your CV more recruiter-friendly with minimal effort.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

- Built with Python and Streamlit  
- Uses natural language processing to understand content  
- Offers feedback on sections, language use, and flow  
- Designed to be simple, fast, and useful for students and professionals alike

---

## 📌 Features

- Upload your resume in seconds  
- Get instant analysis  
- Python-powered NLP  
- Clean and interactive UI with Streamlit  
- Free and accessible online

---

## 💡 Why It Matters

Your first impression is often your resume.  
This tool gives you real, data-driven feedback so you can refine your narrative and present your best self to recruiters.

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack

- Python  
- Streamlit  
- Natural Language Processing  
- Hosted live on Streamlit Cloud
